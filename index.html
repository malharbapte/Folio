The story

In one paragraph

Outbound RVs had a one-line brief and a logo borrowed from its parent. I listened first, wrote the brand down, and let it decide the website.

This site follows that same order. Each part answers the question the previous one raised, so read them in sequence: what I was asked, what I heard, what I decided about the brand, how that became a website, what else I made, and what I learned.
